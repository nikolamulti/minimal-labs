## Variants

### Orientation

The divider can be displayed horizontally or vertically.

<SvelteComponent height={80} url="/divider/orientation" />

```svelte
<!-- Horizontal divider (default) -->
<Divider orientation="wfs-horizontal" />

<!-- Vertical divider -->
<Divider orientation="wfs-vertical" />
```

### Elevation levels

Four elevation levels are available to control the visual weight.

<SvelteComponent height={160} url="/divider/elevation" />

```svelte
<Divider elevation="1" /> <!-- Lightest -->
<Divider elevation="2" />
<Divider elevation="3" />
<Divider elevation="4" /> <!-- Darkest -->
```
